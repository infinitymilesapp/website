About ∞miles

A softer way to keep home organized

∞miles brings belongings, memories, household records, gentle daily reflection, and private family moments together in one cozy home.

It was created with a simple belief: organization should reduce everyday stress, not create more of it.

A tidy home is not about perfection.
It is about knowing where things are and making more room for the moments that truly matter.
Life, gently connected

Everything begins at home

💚 Daily Care

Start softly with Heart Whisper. Notice your mood, write a Tiny Note, or record something you’re grateful for. Personal body check-ins let you keep measurements and notes for your own reference.

🏡 Organization

Build your real home from rooms and spaces to containers and items. Add photos and tags, search quickly, move or archive content, and open scannable containers with QR codes.

💗 Memories

Keep photos, dates, notes, and tags. Link a memory to a treasured belonging, or let it stand on its own. Save meaningful Family Home Moments to your own Memories, too.

🗂️ Files & Receipts

Keep receipts, manuals, and household documents close. Add image or PDF attachments, useful notes, and links to relevant belongings and spaces.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Home Moments

Share photos and everyday stories with members of your shared home. Leave a comment, enjoy a small moment together, and keep family updates in one private space.

☁️ Personal & Shared Home

Switch between personal and shared homes. A shared home can include up to four members, including the owner. Each account includes storage for up to 25 files, subject to a total limit of 25 MB.

How It Works

Three gentle steps

Browse Guest Preview to get a feel for InfinityMiles. Sign in to add, save, or interact with your own content and use shared-home features.

1. Build your home

Add rooms, spaces, containers, and the items you want to find again.

2. Keep what matters connected

Link belongings with memories and files, or scan a QR container to open its contents.

3. Find, reflect, and share

Search your home, take a quiet Daily Care pause, and share private Home Moments with trusted family members.

Start small. Add only what matters today.
Your digital home can grow gently with you.
Good to know

Current availability

InfinityMiles is available on Google Play for Android. The iPhone and iPad version is available as a TestFlight beta.

Three languages, one cozy home.
The language pack supports English, Traditional Chinese (繁體中文), and Spanish (Español). It is in Android internal testing and iOS TestFlight testing. The public Android update is coming soon. In the language-pack builds, switch languages in Settings → App Preferences.

The app is free to use, with no ads. Optional Support Maggie tips help support its independent creator.

Cloud saving is active. Full backup and restore, and Nearby Community, are not currently available.

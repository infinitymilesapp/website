About ∞miles

A softer way to keep home organized

∞miles brings belongings, memories, household records, gentle daily reflection, and private family moments together in one cozy home.

It was created with a simple belief: organization should reduce everyday stress, not create more of it.

A tidy home is not about perfection.
It is about knowing where things are and making more room for the moments that truly matter.
Current V1

Everything begins at home

💚 Digital Companion

Check in with your mood, receive a daily Heart Whisper, save favorites in the Treasure Box, and spend a quiet moment with Otter, Bear, Puppy, or Bunny.

🏡 Organization

Build your real home from rooms and spaces to containers and items. Add photos and tags, search quickly, move or archive content, and open scannable containers with QR codes.

💗 Memories

Keep photos, dates, notes, and tags, then link a memory directly to the belonging it celebrates. Family Home Moments can also be saved into Memory.

🗂️ Paper Vault

Keep useful household records with dates, vendors, amounts, tags, linked items, and PDF or image attachments—up to 10 attachments per record.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Home Moments

Share private moments with members of your family home. Nearby Community is a future feature and is not active in V1.

☁️ Personal & Shared Home

Switch between personal and shared homes, with cloud saving active. Current accounts include up to 50 MB or 50 files per user and up to 4 members in a shared home.

How It Works

Three gentle steps

1. Build your home

Add rooms, spaces, containers, and the items you want to find again.

2. Keep what matters connected

Link belongings with memories and household records, or scan a QR container to open its contents.

3. Find, reflect, and share

Search your home, enjoy a Heart Whisper, and share private Home Moments with trusted family members.

Start small. Add only what matters today.
Your digital home can grow gently with you.
Good to know

Current availability

English is the current default language. Traditional Chinese is planned as the next language alongside iOS support.

Cloud saving is active; full restore is not yet available. Cozy Premium, AI mascot mode, Nearby Community, and downloadable yearly Heart Whisper packs are future possibilities, not active V1 features.

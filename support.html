Common questions

What can I do in Guest Preview?

You can browse the preview. Sign in to create, save, comment, or use shared-home features.

How do I change the language?

In language-pack builds, go to Settings → App Preferences and choose English, Traditional Chinese, or Spanish. The language pack is currently in testing; the public Android update is coming soon.

How much storage is included?

Each account includes up to 25 files, subject to a total limit of 25 MB. Check the usage shown in your app before adding attachments or photos.

Who can see Family Home Moments?

Moments are shared within your home. Invite only people you trust. A shared home supports up to four members, including its owner.

Do Support Maggie tips unlock features?

No. Tips are optional ways to support the creator. They do not unlock features or increase storage. If a purchase has a problem, contact support with the product name and purchase date, without including payment-card details.

Can I restore a full backup?

Cloud saving is active, but full backup and restore is not currently available.

How do I request account deletion?

Sign in, then open Settings → Account → Delete Account and follow the instructions to submit a request. If you cannot access your account, you can request deletion by email. Visit our account-deletion page for details.

Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Account

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

You can request deletion of your InfinityMiles account and associated data directly in the app.

Request Deletion in the App

  1. Sign in to the InfinityMiles account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions to submit your deletion request.

This submits a request for processing; it does not mean deletion is completed immediately.

If You Cannot Access Your Account

You can also request deletion by emailing love.infinitymiles@gmail.com.

Use the subject Delete My InfinityMiles Account. Include your display name, the email associated with the account if known, and whether you used Google or Apple sign-in so we can help identify the correct account. We may need to verify ownership before processing the request.

Please do not send passwords or sign-in verification codes.

What Will Be Deleted

When your request is completed, we will delete account data associated with your ∞miles account, which may include:

  • Account profile information used by ∞miles
  • Home spaces
  • Rooms and storage locations
  • Inventory items
  • Memory entries
  • Shared home membership information
  • Private family Home Moment posts you created
  • Uploaded photos associated with your account, where applicable

What May Be Kept Temporarily

Some information may be retained temporarily if required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or service-reliability purposes. Any retained information will be kept only as long as necessary.

Deletion Timeline

Most deletion requests are completed within 30 days after we verify the account request.

Deleting Individual Content

You can use the available Delete controls in the app to remove individual content while keeping your account. Deleting an item or entry is separate from requesting deletion of your entire account. Contact support if you need help.
